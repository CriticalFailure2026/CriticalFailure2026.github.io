FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Critical Failure based?

Critical Failure is proudly based in Bali, Indonesia. We work closely with local manufacturers and oversee every step of production to ensure premium quality before shipping worldwide.

How long has Critical Failure been running for?

Critical Failure was founded in 2026. Since the beginning, we have focused on creating underground streetwear inspired by hardcore music, D.I.Y Do It Yourself Energy, raw design, balinese culture, and modern streetwear 100% local people from Bali, Indonesia.

Do you have a physical store?

Currently, Critical Failure is exclusively available online. While we do not have a permanent physical store yet, we occasionally plan pop-up releases and special events. Follow ourFollow our Instagram @critical.failure2026 for future updates.

Who designs the collections?

Every collection is designed independently in Sleepy Boy Design house. Our designs are heavily inspired by underground culture, metal aesthetics, streetwear, and dystopian visuals.

How can I get featured on the Critical Failure page?

We love seeing how people wear Critical Failure. Post your fit on Follow our Instagram @critical.failure2026 Selected photos may be reposted on our story or feed.
